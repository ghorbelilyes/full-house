# Full House — Image & Icon Generation Tasks

## Reference Logo

The Full House logo has these elements:
- **Circular swirl**: 4 curved arcs forming a circle — 2 orange (`#E87A1E`), 2 black (`#1A1A1A`), alternating
- **House**: centered inside the swirl — black roof peak, orange secondary roof line, small black 4-pane window
- **Text "FULL HOUSE"**: bold/heavy weight, orange (`#E87A1E`), underlined
- **Text "Electricity & Security"**: serif italic, black (`#1A1A1A`)
